03 · Backgrounds

Cream — default

#f7f4ec · Most posts. Text ink. Keyword lime. For educational, tips, creator-facing content. Shapes: pastel, full-opacity paper-cutout from brand kit.

Dark — brand-facing

#000 · Text cream. Keyword lime. Distinctive shapes (flower, drop, sparkle, cone) with light blur (4–8px) and full-opacity vivid colors, bleeding off edges. Evernote-style depth: shape silhouette stays defined, only edges feather.

Lime — high energy / CTA

04 · Typography hierarchy

Five-level scale — readable at all sizes

Preview cards in this document are scaled ~5.5× smaller than the 1080-px production canvas. Sizes below are given as preview px → production pt (×5.5 for carousel/post, ×6.17 for story).

1. Hero number (stat-only slides) — Poppins 900

50–52px → 275–290px production (≈140pt)
Tracking -0.04em · line-height 1.0. Use for "$1,340" · "80%" · "7.4M" · "210+". One per slide. Color: lime on cream/dark, ink on lime.

2. Headline (display) — Poppins 900

Cover: 23–25px → ~140pt
Stories: 17–21px → ~120pt
Carousels / Posts (internal): 14–17px → ~95pt
Tracking -0.04em · line-height 1.05–1.15. Always exactly one keyword wrapped in <span class="k"> (lime). Min production size: 48pt.

3. Subhead (supporting line) — Poppins 700

11–13px → ~65pt
Tracking -0.01em · line-height 1.2. Use directly under the hero number ("earned last month") or as a single-line supporting clause under the headline. Min production size: 32pt.

4. Body (paragraph) — Inter 500

9–10px → ~55pt
Line-height 1.45. Used for explanatory copy, tips descriptions, captions. Max 2 lines on covers, 4 lines on internal slides. Min production size: 26pt — never smaller.

5. Eyebrow & meta — JetBrains Mono 500

7–8px → ~44pt
letter-spacing: 0.1em · ALL CAPS. The ONLY eyebrow text printed on a live card is the URL-as-CTA (USECREABLE.COM) on stat/quote slides, or SWIPE → on covers. Section/topic labels (FOR BRANDS, THE PROBLEM…) are internal classification only — never printed (§11.5). Min production size: 22pt.

Character budgets

Cover headline: max 40 chars · Internal slide: max 60 chars · Story: max 35 chars · Static post: max 50 chars · Body paragraph: max 140 chars (≈ 2 short lines on cards, 4 lines on production)

05 · Margins & safe zones (per format)

Universal rules

Every format has its own safe zone because Instagram's UI overlays different chrome on each. Anything critical (logo · headline · body · CTA · key data) MUST live inside the safe zone. Decorative shapes can bleed outside.
Composition rule: always center the critical block vertically + horizontally within the safe zone. Edge-aligned (left or right) only on Carousel Educational slides.
Logo: always top-center, mark only (.ucm).

1. Reels — 1080 × 1920 (9:16)

The most aggressive overlay. Instagram chrome covers:
  Top: ~250px (≈14%) — app quick actions
  Bottom: 350–450px — caption + audio title
  Right: vertical strip — like / comment / share buttons
Safe zone: top 250px · bottom 450px · right 220px · left 60px
Critical block: center column 760px wide, vertically centered between safe boundaries. Everything text-related stays in this column.
Logo top: 290px from canvas top (just below the top overlay).
CTA bottom: 470px from canvas bottom (just above the caption overlay).

2. Stories — 1080 × 1920 (9:16)

Less aggressive than Reels but still trimmed top + bottom:
  Top: ~250px — progress bar + account name + close button
  Bottom: ~250px — reply field + "Send message" CTA
Safe zone: 250px top + bottom · 60px left + right
Critical block: centered horizontally + vertically inside the safe zone.
Logo top: 280px from canvas top.
CTA bottom: 280px from canvas bottom.

3. Feed posts — Carousel 1080 × 1350 (4:5) · Static 1080 × 1080 (1:1) · Grid 1080 × 1440 (3:4)

Caption renders below the asset — minimal overlay. The risk is grid-thumbnail cropping in profile view.
  Carousel 4:5: safe margin 10% from each edge (108px) → logo, headline, CTA all live inside the inner 864×1080 area.
  Static 1:1: safe margin 8% (86px) → inner 908×908.
  3:4 (grid preview): the top/bottom 12% of a 4:5 post gets cropped in the grid — never place critical content there if it'll appear in a profile grid.
Logo top: 8% from canvas top (≈86–108px).
CTA bottom: 8% from canvas bottom.
Educational (left-aligned): text-left from 14% from left edge, text-right margin 12%.

Internal spacing (all formats, applied within safe zone)

Headline ↔ logo: ≥ 16px gap
Body ↔ headline: 6–10px gap
CTA pill ↔ bottom of safe zone: 16–24px
Eyebrow USECREABLE.COM ↔ bottom of safe zone: 10–14px (smaller because eyebrow is itself a CTA-replacement)
Screenshot ↔ headline: 12–16px · screenshot stays within safe zone, never bleeds
Shapes: may extend off-canvas; visible silhouette stays at least 40px from the headline bounding box (§06.1 §7).

Preview cards in this document

The mobile preview cards on the right (175×311 for Stories, 196×245 for Carousels, 215×215 for Posts) are scaled-down proofs. The proportions, alignment, and relative spacing match the 1080-px production canvas. To convert: multiply preview px × 6.17 (Stories), × 5.51 (Carousels), × 5.02 (Posts).

06 · Paper-cutout shapes (full set from brand kit)

12 shapes — pastel for Creators, blur for Brands

Creator content (cream bg): Use all 12 shapes in pastel colors — yellow, lavender, pink-soft, orange, lime-light. Opacity 45–80%. Full color, no blur. 2–3 per template at edges.
Brand content (dark bg): Use the 12 brand shapes with filter: blur(4–8px) at full opacity, vivid brand colors (lime, lavender, yellow, orange, magenta). Position at edges, bleeding off the card. Evernote-style: defined silhouette, feathered edge. 2–3 shapes per card.
Shapes: Starburst, Amoeba blob, Solid circle, Capsule pill, Sparkle, Flower, Soft blob, Squircle, Diamond, Star, Eye, Drop

06.1 · Shape × Text — contrast & legibility rules

The text must always win. Shapes serve the composition, never compete with it.

1. Never overlap headline or body text. Shapes live at corners or edges with negative offsets, bleeding off-canvas. The visible portion stays in the outer ~30% of the card. CTA pill and logo mark are an exception — they have their own background/border and can have shapes near or behind them without losing legibility.
2. Z-index hierarchy is non-negotiable. Text + logo + CTA = z-index: 10. Shapes = z-index: 0–5. Never the other way.
3. Color collision is forbidden. A shape may NEVER share its color with a text element close to it.
    ✗ lime keyword + lime shape adjacent → unreadable
    ✗ yellow stat on lime bg with yellow shape behind it → unreadable
    ✓ lime keyword on dark + lavender / yellow / magenta / orange shapes near text
    ✓ yellow stat on lime + ink / lavender / pink shapes near text
4. Safe palettes per background:
    Dark bg + lime keyword: shapes adjacent to text = lavender, yellow, orange, magenta. Lime allowed only as small accent at the farthest corner.
    Cream bg + lime keyword: shapes near text = yellow, lavender, magenta, pink-soft, orange. Lime allowed only diluted (lime-100/200) or far corner.
    Lime bg + ink text: shapes near text = yellow, magenta, ink-tint. No lime variations near text.
5. Blur is a separator. Heavier blur (6–8px) on big shapes pushes them visually back. Sharper edges (2–4px) only on small accent shapes that are FAR from text.
6. Squint test. Squint at the card. If a shape's color blurs into a text element of the same hue, change the shape color or move it further. Text legibility ALWAYS wins.
7. Headline safe zone. No shape may have its visible silhouette within 40px of the headline's bounding box.
8. Numeric shape no-go zones — measured in pixels of the production canvas. Any visible shape pixel (including the soft edge of a halo / blur) MUST fall outside the rectangle below. Hard rule, not interpretable.
    Stories (1080 × 1920) — no-go = x: 60–1020, y: 400–1520 (text + CTA column)
    Carousels (1080 × 1350) — no-go = x: 60–1020, y: 300–950 (text block) + y: 1080–1220 only when CTA pill present (skip if eyebrow only — eyebrow doesn't need protection)
    Static Posts (1080 × 1080) — no-go = x: 60–1020, y: 200–850 (text block) + y: 920–1040 when CTA pill present
How to check: draw a bounding box around every shape (including its blurred halo / glow). The bounding box must NOT intersect the no-go rectangle. If it does, shrink the shape or move it further off-canvas. Logo and CTA pill backgrounds protect their own area — shapes may pass behind them.

07 · FOR BRANDS — design language

Outcome-driven · dark · crisp shapes + glow halo

Background: #000 (dark). Lime allowed only as keyword in headlines.
Logo: mark variant .m.ld (white on dark).
Eyebrow: FOR BRANDS is an internal label only — NOT printed on the card (see §11.5). Lead with the headline.
Tone: Outcome-driven, B2B, confident, scale-focused. Speak to decision-makers and marketers. Lead with numbers, results, and infrastructure.
Copy examples: "Your content, in every feed." · "80% less production time." · "312 creators. 7.4M views." · "Scale your content. Today." · "One brief. Hundreds of feeds."
Use case: Brand statements, stat reveals, social proof to marketers, feature announcements, CTA finale on dark backgrounds.

Brand shape system — crisp + glow halo (Evernote-style)

Three shape treatments combine on a single card. Use 2–3 per card.

A. Crisp shape + glow halo (the dominant treatment) — sharp silhouette in saturated neon color, no blur, partially cut by the frame edge. Behind it: a larger copy of the same shape, same color, with filter: blur(70–80px) at production scale (~13–15px at preview scale), opacity 0.7–0.85, offset slightly so the glow extends asymmetrically into the dark. Creates the "colored neon object with halo" effect from Evernote.
B. Light-blur shape (the supporting treatment) — the original 12 brand shapes (flower, drop, eye, diamond, star, sparkle, capsule, squircle, soft blob, amoeba blob, solid circle, cone) with filter: blur(4–8px) at preview / 22–44px at production. Full opacity, vivid colors. Silhouette stays clearly readable. Use for accents in corners far from the main crisp shape.
C. Full-blur atmospheric blob (rare, max 1 per card) — a single shape with filter: blur(50–80px) at preview / 140px+ at production. The silhouette dissolves into a pure colored glow. Use only for background atmosphere in negative space far from the focal point. Never as the main brand element.

Colors — neon palette only: lime (#a4d435, #cdf03c), yellow (#FBBF24), lavender (#A78BFA), orange (#F97316), magenta (#EC4899).
Asymmetric glow — offset the halo so MORE of the glow falls AWAY from the frame edge and INTO the dark composition (where there's room), and LESS falls outside the frame. The glow is doing the visual work; it should be visible.
Combining treatments — typical card: 1× crisp+halo (the hero shape, dominant corner) + 1× light-blur (smaller accent in opposite corner) + optionally 1× full-blur atmosphere (background mass behind the hero).

09 · Screenshot variants

Two variants based on text length

Short text (≤ 80 chars): Screenshot takes 70% of card height. Text small underneath.
Long text (> 80 chars): Screenshot takes 40% of card height. Text occupies more space above.
Mock browser chrome with 3 dots + URL always present. Slight rotation (1–2°). Light variant on cream, dark variant on dark bg.

10 · Alignment

Centered by default — left-aligned ONLY for educational

Default = centered. Logo top-center; headline, sub-head and body all centered horizontally. This applies to: covers, statements, stat slides, quotes, feature slides, CTA finales, and every Story and Static Post.
Exception = educational. ONLY genuinely educational slides — numbered tips lists and multi-paragraph teaching content (the C3 type) — use left-aligned text. Logo stays top-center even then.
Rule of thumb: if it's not a tips list or a multi-paragraph how-to, center it.

11 · Language

Bilingual: EN + IT

Same templates, same layout — only copy changes. Maintain character limits across languages.

11.5 · Eyebrows — what may be printed on a card

No topic / section labels on published posts

Never print section or topic eyebrows like THE PROBLEM, THE SHIFT, THE VALUE, USE CASES, FOR BRANDS, FOR CREATORS on a finished post. They are internal classification only (used to organize this library and to tell the designer which language to apply) — they add clutter and never ship on the artwork.
The only eyebrow text allowed on a live card:
  • USECREABLE.COM — on stat / quote slides, in place of a CTA pill (§02).
  • SWIPE → — on carousel cover slides only.
Everything else leads with the headline. No kicker, no category tag.

12 · Archetype decision tree (deterministic)

Given input → exactly one template archetype

Apply rules in order. The first that matches wins.
1. Input contains a hero number (≥4 chars, e.g. "80%", "$1,340", "7.4M", "312") AND nothing else dominant → Stat
2. Input is a quote with attribution ("— Maya, AI creator") OR cites a real person's result → Social proof / Quote
3. Input is a CTA verb ("Start", "Scale", "Get", "Upload") + outcome promise, last slide of sequence → CTA finale
4. Input mentions a product feature/screen ("dashboard", "briefs view", "earnings", "campaign") AND has a screenshot/data asset → Feature + screenshot (or Dashboard if the screenshot IS the hero)
5. Input is a numbered list, tips, "5 ways to...", "how to..." → Educational — Tips list
6. Input is multi-paragraph teaching content ("Brands post briefs. You create...") → Educational — Text
7. Input is the first slide of a sequence / opens a topic → Cover
8. Default → Statement

Format selection

Caller specifies one of Story / Carousel / Static Post and the audience (Brand / Creator). Combined with the archetype above, this picks ONE template card from the matrix. No ambiguity.

12.5 · Responsive cover typography

Short cover copy → bigger type, never filler

When a carousel cover has little copy it must NOT look empty. Fill the canvas with typographic scale, never with extra cards, mockups, UI, avatars, feeds or decorative filler. Count headline + body characters (spaces included), then pick a layout band. Keep logo top-center, headline dominant, body under it, CTA pill bottom-center, 2–3 shapes at the edges, one lime keyword, text inside safe area.

≤ 110 chars — "large cover": headline +15–25% over standard (production ~165–185pt), body +15–25% (~62–72pt), headline line-height 0.95–1.02, body 1.25–1.35. The text block carries the slide.
111–160 chars — "standard": headline ~135–155pt, body ~52–60pt, headline line-height 1.05–1.12, body 1.35–1.45.
161–210 chars — "compact": headline ~110–130pt, body ~44–52pt, headline line-height 1.08–1.15, body 1.4–1.5.
> 210 chars: do NOT shrink below system minimums (§17) — shorten the copy, split into more slides, or change archetype.
Fill the void only with: larger headline scale, larger body scale, tighter line-height, a more dominant text block, and a small vertical re-centering of the block. Nothing else.

12.6 · Text always outweighs the visual

When a slide pairs copy with a supporting visual

On any slide that has BOTH text and a visual element (a diagram, a "1 → many" graphic, a small mock, an illustrative grid — anything that isn't the headline/body/CTA), the text is the protagonist and the visual is only support. Target weight ≈ 70% text / 30% visual.

Order of work: 1) strengthen the copy first; 2) then set the typographic scale (headline dominant, body clear); 3) only at the end size/position the visual; 4) if the slide still feels empty, do NOT add more graphics — adjust text size, rhythm and vertical position instead.
The visual must: sit below / secondary to the text block, be scaled down so it never competes with the headline, stay centered, keep its labels small and quiet, and never grow into a dashboard/feed/mockup. It illustrates the concept — it doesn't carry the slide.
Reading test: the message must be readable from the text alone in 2–3 seconds; the visual just reinforces it.

13 · Screenshot variant selector

Char count → variant

Count all non-screenshot text on the slide (headline + body, exclude logo + eyebrow + CTA).
≤ 80 characters total → Screenshot variant A: screenshot 70% of card, headline below screenshot, short.
> 80 characters total → Screenshot variant B: screenshot 40% of card, headline + body above screenshot.
Threshold is hard. Round up. If exactly 80 → variant A.

15 · Headline keyword & line-break rules

Exactly one keyword per headline, deterministic placement

Length: 1–3 words consecutive, wrapped in <span class="k">.
Position: ALWAYS the last meaningful phrase of the last sentence — usually the punchline noun or outcome.
Color rule:
    Cream bg → keyword = lime #8fc01a
    Dark bg → keyword = lime #a4d435 (slightly brighter)
    Lime bg → keyword = ink (rare) OR no keyword
Examples: "Your content, in every feed." · "She made $1,340 last month..." · "Start earning. This week." · "5 ways to earn more."
Line break: only with explicit <br> between two clauses ("80%
less production time"). Never let the keyword span a line break.

16 · Word budgets per archetype × format

Hard caps — if exceeded, apply §17 overflow protocol

Budgets count every word in the critical block (headline + subhead + body). Hero numbers and the CTA pill / eyebrow URL are excluded — they're fixed. "H" = headline words, "S" = subhead words, "B" = body words.

Statement — Stories 8–10 H · Carousels 8–10 H · Posts 9–12 H
Stat — number + 4 S + 6 B (same all formats)
Quote / Social proof — Stories 4 attribution + 14 H + 6 B = 24 max · Carousels 24 · Posts 28
Cover — 6–8 H + SWIPE eyebrow (Carousels only; Stories + Posts don't use Cover)
Educational text — Carousels: 5 H + 30 B = 35 max · NOT used in Stories or Static Posts
Tips list — Carousels: 5 H + 5 items × 8 B = 45 max · NOT used in Stories or Static Posts
Feature + screenshot ≤80 — 6–8 H, no body, screenshot 70% (all formats)
Feature + screenshot >80 — 6 H + 20 B = 26 max, screenshot 40% (all formats)
CTA finale — 5 H + 6 B = 11 max (all formats)
Dashboard — screenshot + 8 B caption · Static Posts only

17 · Overflow protocol

If the copy exceeds the budget — apply steps in order, never skip

Step A · Tighten copy. Strip adverbs, articles, "really" / "actually". Active verbs. One sentence instead of two. If it fits → done.
Step B · Split into 2 slides. Only if Tighten doesn't bring it under budget.
  Stories → 2 consecutive stories. No "1/2 2/2" indicator (clean).
  Carousels → add one extra slide between cover and CTA.
  Static Posts → convert to a 2-slide carousel (never accept overflow on a single post).
Step C · Change archetype. If even split doesn't fit (e.g. 200-char quote in a Quote slide), force a switch to Educational text (Carousel) or move the long-form to a blog post and use a cover-link card on social.

Minimum font sizes — never go below. If the copy still doesn't fit at the minimums below, you MUST go to Step B, not shrink further:
  Headline ≥ 48pt · Subhead ≥ 32pt · Body ≥ 26pt · Eyebrow ≥ 22pt (production scale, 1080-px canvas)

18.5 · Component anatomies (reusable schemas)

Stat slide — fixed stack

Always uses this 4-layer stack, in this exact order:
1. Hero number — Poppins 900, lime (cream / dark bg) or ink (lime bg). Production: 275–320pt. Preview: 48–52px. Single line.
2. Subhead — Poppins 700, ink (cream / lime bg) or cream (dark bg). Production: 76pt. Preview: 13–14.5px. One short line describing the number ("views in 30 days", "earned last month", "creators already earning").
3. Caption (optional) — Inter 500, mt color, opacity .55–.85. Production: 44pt. Preview: 9.5px. One short qualifier line ("across 312 creators · one brief", "— a real creator on Usecreable"). Omit if hero + subhead are self-sufficient.
4. Eyebrow URL — JetBrains Mono 500, mt color, opacity .6. Production: 44pt. Preview: 7px. Always "USECREABLE.COM" all caps. Never replace with pill CTA on stat slides (§02 rule).
Stack centered vertically + horizontally. No CTA pill. No bullet, no icon, no extra accent text.

Tips list (5-item) — bullet + row anatomy

Left-aligned column of 5 numbered rows. Always exactly 5 (never 4, never 6). Anatomy per row:
Bullet box — 16×16px (preview) / 88×88px (production), background lime, border-radius 4px (preview) / 22px (production). Contains the row number.
Bullet number — Poppins 800, ink color. Preview: 9px. Production: 50pt. Centered in box.
Row text — Inter 500, ink (cream bg) or mt (dark bg), opacity .75. Preview: 9.5px. Production: 52pt. Vertically center-aligned with the bullet.
Bullet-to-text gap — 5px (preview) / 28px (production).
Row-to-row gap — 4px (preview) / 30px (production). Tight on purpose: 5 rows must fit alongside a headline at the top.
Headline above list — 1 line max, ends with a 1-word lime keyword ("5 ways to earn more." / "5 steps to ship a brief."). Gap to first row: 9px preview / 50px production.

19 · Production checklist (apply in order)

Run before any card is finalized

1. Format + audience + archetype received from caller → pick template cell from matrix.
2. Count chars (headline + body) → pick screenshot variant if applicable (§13).
3. Count words in the critical block — if over the budget for that archetype × format (§16), apply overflow protocol §19 BEFORE laying out.
4. Identify keyword (last phrase, 1–3 words) → wrap in <span class="k"> (§15).
5. Pick shape combo by card index mod combo-count for the audience + bg (§14). Verify color collision (§06.1).
6. Place logo, headline, body, screenshot/CTA per §05 spacing.
7. CTA: pill by default; eyebrow USECREABLE.COM if hero number/quote present (§02).
8. Run shape×text squint test (§06.1 §6) — if collision, swap shape color from the safe palette (§06.1 §4).
9. Verify font min sizes (Headline 48pt · Subhead 32pt · Body 26pt · Eyebrow 22pt) — if anything is below min, apply §17 split.
10. Lime variant? Only for Creator + use cases in §18. Never Brand.
11. Done. Card is unambiguous.
